What this test contains

25 questions on Unit 1 (Asymptotic Analysis & Recurrences), Set 1 of 2. All four subtopics are covered: big-O / Ω / Θ, the Master Theorem cases, the recursion-tree method and the substitution method. Composition: 15 MCQ, 5 MSQ, 5 NAT; difficulty 10 easy, 10 medium, 5 hard; 60 minutes, 35 marks. MCQ carries 1 mark, MSQ and NAT carry 2 marks, there is no negative marking, and an MSQ scores only when the exact set of correct options is selected. This is a practice marking scheme, not the official GATE scheme.

Concept map used to build the questions

  • Big-O / Ω / Θ. The formal constant-factor definitions and what each one permits; growth ordering settled by ratios rather than by intuition (n/log n above √n but below n); dominance of the fastest-growing term in a sum; why Θ is preserved by addition but destroyed by exponentiation; and quasi-polynomial growth such as n^(log n) overtaking every fixed n^k.
  • Master Theorem cases. Reading off a, b and the homogeneous term n^(log_b a); the three polynomial-gap cases; the extended case that adds a logarithm when f(n) already carries a log^k n factor; and the regularity condition that Case 3 actually requires.
  • Recursion tree method. Per-level cost against tree height; leaf count a^(log_b n); unbalanced trees whose two branch depths differ only by a constant factor; subtract-and-conquer trees that unroll into arithmetic series; and why leaf cost never dominates internal cost.
  • Substitution method. Guessing a bound and certifying it by induction; locating the constant condition from the inductive residual (the −cn term); and strengthening a failing hypothesis with a lower-order term instead of abandoning the bound.
