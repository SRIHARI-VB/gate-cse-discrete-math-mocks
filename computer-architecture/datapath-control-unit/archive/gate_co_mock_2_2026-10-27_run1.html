What this test contains

25 questions on Unit 2 (CPU Control Design & Data Path), Set 1 of 2, the first 25 of the 50-question allowance for this unit. All four subtopics are covered: hardwired control (7), microprogrammed control (6), ALU and datapath timing (6), and bus architectures (6). Composition: 15 MCQ, 5 MSQ, 5 NAT; difficulty 10 easy, 10 medium, 5 hard; 60 minutes, 35 marks. MCQ carries 1 mark, MSQ and NAT carry 2 marks, there is no negative marking, and an MSQ scores only when the exact set of correct options is selected. This is a practice marking scheme, not the official GATE scheme.

Concept map used to build the questions

  • Hardwired control. control-signal generation from opcode, flags and timing state; the sequence counter and its decoder; field widths for opcodes and states; why combinational control is fast but rigid.
  • Microprogrammed control. the control store and control word; horizontal versus vertical encoding and the decoding cost; sequencing fields and microprogram-counter width; capacity arithmetic; microcode as the way to extend an instruction set.
  • ALU and datapath timing. function-select codes; flag generation such as the zero detector; the register-to-register critical path on a single bus; ripple-carry delay; clock-period arithmetic with clk-to-Q, logic, multiplexer and setup delays; the effect of pipeline registers.
  • Bus architectures. single, two and three-bus datapaths; bus width versus address width; synchronous versus asynchronous timing; bandwidth arithmetic with multi-cycle transfers; daisy-chain, independent-request and polling arbitration.
