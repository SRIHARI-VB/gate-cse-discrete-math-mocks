What this test contains

25 questions on Unit 1 (Layering, Physical & Framing), Set 1 of 2. All four subtopics are covered: the OSI and TCP/IP models and what each layer owns; transmission delay, propagation delay and the bandwidth-delay product; framing by character count, flag bytes and bit stuffing; and error detection by CRC, Internet checksum and Hamming codes. Composition: 15 MCQ, 5 MSQ, 5 NAT; difficulty 10 easy, 10 medium, 5 hard; 60 minutes, 35 marks. MCQ carries 1 mark, MSQ and NAT carry 2 marks, there is no negative marking, and an MSQ scores only when the exact set of correct options is selected. This is a practice marking scheme, not the official GATE scheme.

Concept map used to build the questions

  • OSI and TCP/IP models. The seven OSI layers against the four TCP/IP layers, which OSI functions the TCP/IP application layer absorbs, the device that belongs to each layer (hub, switch, router), what a header at each layer carries, and the scope of a check — a frame check sequence covers one hop, a transport checksum covers the whole path.
  • Transmission delays and the bandwidth-delay product. L/R against d/s; why only one of them depends on packet size and only the other on distance; store-and-forward across a chain of links; the bandwidth-delay product as the number of bits in flight; and the bottleneck link that fixes a path's throughput.
  • Framing: bit and byte stuffing. Character-count framing and its single point of failure; the flag byte with an escape byte, including the escape of the escape byte itself; the flag bit pattern 01111110 and the insert-a-zero-after-five-1s rule, with de-stuffing at the receiver; and the counter discipline that makes a stuffing trace reproducible.
  • Error detection: CRC, checksum, Hamming. Hamming distance and the detect-d−1 / correct-⌊(d−1)/2⌋ pair; the Hamming parity-count inequality m + r + 1 ≤ 2r; polynomial division over GF(2) with XOR instead of subtraction; the two CRC guarantees and the guarantee it does not give; and the Internet checksum's end-around carry.
