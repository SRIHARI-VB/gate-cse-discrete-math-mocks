What this test contains

25 questions on Unit 1 (Linear Algebra: Matrices & Systems), Set 1 of 2. All four subtopics are covered: matrix operations, rank and nullity, systems of linear equations and Gauss elimination. Composition: 15 MCQ, 5 MSQ, 5 NAT; difficulty 10 easy, 10 medium, 5 hard; 60 minutes, 35 marks. MCQ carries 1 mark, MSQ and NAT carry 2 marks, there is no negative marking, and an MSQ scores only when the exact set of correct options is selected. This is a practice marking scheme, not the official GATE scheme.

Concept map used to build the questions

  • Matrix operations. Conformability of a product and the order of the result; transpose and inverse both reversing order while transpose distributes over sums; the determinant rules det(AB) = det(A)det(B), det(kA) = kⁿdet(A) and det(adj A) = det(A)^(n−1); zero divisors, where AB = 0 forces singularity but not a zero factor; and why A² = I does not force A = ±I.
  • Rank & nullity. The rank–nullity theorem rank(A) + nullity(A) = n counted over columns, never rows; equality of row rank and column rank; the ambient space of the column space being ℝ^m; and projections, where A² = A makes the trace equal the rank.
  • Systems of linear equations. Consistency settled by comparing rank(A) with rank([A | b]); uniqueness settled by nullity; the three outcomes for a singular square system; and why a homogeneous system with more unknowns than equations always has a non-trivial solution.
  • Gauss elimination. Reading rank off the echelon form rather than the first column; forward elimination against Gauss–Jordan reduction; partial pivoting and the n³/3 operation count; and the exact condition under which an LU factorisation exists without row interchanges.
